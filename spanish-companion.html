HOW TO TALK WITH CHARLIE
  1. Allow microphone access if your browser asks. Use Chrome or Edge.
  2. The ring glows cyan and pulses while Charlie is talking — just listen.
  3. Press and hold the microphone button while you speak your answer out loud, in Spanish, then release it when you're done.
  4. The ring turns gold and shows ESCUCHANDO while you're holding it down.
  5. Charlie will respond, then it's your turn again — press and hold the mic to reply.
